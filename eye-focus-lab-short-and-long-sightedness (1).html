What the rays show

Without correction, the eye bends the incoming rays too strongly. They meet before the retina, so distant vision is blurred. A concave lens spreads the rays before they enter the eye.

This is a simplified, not-to-scale eye model. Lens strength is measured in diopters; the drawn thickness is a visual cue for curvature and power—not a medical prescription. $P=1/f$, with focal length $f$ in metres.

Try changing the eye defect first, then add a lens and tune its strength until the rays meet on the retina.
